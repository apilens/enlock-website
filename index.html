Questions

Does Slomo upload my documents?

No. Slomo has no servers and makes no network requests. Data leaves your phone only when you share something or save a backup yourself.

What if I lose my phone?

Restore your encrypted backup on a new phone with your recovery key. Without a backup and the key, the data can't be recovered, by you or by us.

I forgot my PIN.

Enter your recovery key to set a new one. That's why the key is shown once during setup. Keep it somewhere safe, away from your phone.

Which documents does it read?

Aadhaar (including VID), PAN, credit and debit cards, driving licences, voter ID and passports. Anything else can be stored with a template and filled in by hand.

How do I get help?

Email support@slomo.dev. For anything else, say hello@slomo.dev. Please never send document numbers or photos of your cards.

Is it on Android?

iPhone first. Scanning and reading use Apple's on-device VisionKit and Vision frameworks.
